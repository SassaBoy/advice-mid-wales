Our appointment times are Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays: 10:30am - 2:30pm

Please get in touch if these times are not convenient and we will try our best to offer an alternative.

Although we do offer walk-in appointments, it is advisable to contact us in advance to ensure that we have availability.

You can phone us on 01654 700 192. If there's no answer during our opening hours, we're probably with clients so please leave a message and number and one of our team will get back to you as soon as possible.

Or you can email us at enquiries@advicemidwales.org.uk
